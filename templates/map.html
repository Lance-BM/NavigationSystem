About Loyola Walkers

🎯 Why We Exist

In Brgy. Loyola Heights, not all routes are safe. Poorly lit streets, blocked sidewalks, and areas prone to flooding make it risky for students, residents, and commuters to get around.

Our navigation system exists to help the Loyola Heights community find the safest route, avoiding dangerous or high-risk areas, so everyone can walk, commute, and travel with greater peace of mind.

Whether you're heading to school, going home, or running errands, we want to make sure you reach your destination safely.

⚡ How It Works

Our app uses real-time data and community reports to analyze the safest routes in Brgy. Loyola Heights. It considers factors like sidewalk conditions, crime-prone areas, and flood risks to guide you through the best and safest path.

Our algorithm:

  • Uses updated safety data from residents and local reports
  • Avoids hazardous areas like poorly lit streets and flood-prone zones
  • Suggests the safest walking or commuting route on an interactive map

Safe routes. A more secure Loyola Heights.

🔧 Our Quiet Route Algorithm

1

Analyze Your Route

We calculate the real-world distance between your points and download a street network sized to fit — short trips get a tight graph, long routes get a wider one.

2

Assign Noise Costs

Each street gets a noise score from 1× (footpath) to 8× (motorway) based on its road type. Unknown roads default to 2.5× — never silently ignored.

3

Find Quiet Path

Our pathfinding algorithm calculates the route with the lowest total noise cost, not the shortest distance.

4

Navigate Peacefully

Follow the route on your map through the quietest streets available. If a location can't be found or no path exists, you'll get a clear, friendly message — not a crash.

Noise Cost Breakdown

🛣️ Motorways & Trunks
Cost: 6–8×
🚗 Primary Roads
Cost: 3–5×
🛤️ Secondary & Tertiary
Cost: 1.5–2.5×
🏘️ Residential, Paths & Footways
Cost: 1–1.5×
