🧩 About Sensory Impact Navigation

🎯 Why We Exist

Modern navigation apps prioritize speed and distance, but they ignore something that affects our mental health, productivity, and wellbeing: noise pollution.

Studies show that continuous exposure to high noise levels causes stress, fatigue, and long-term health issues. Sensory Impact Navigation changes that by helping you find routes that preserve your peace of mind.

Whether you're commuting to work, going for a walk, or exploring your city, you deserve to travel through quieter, more pleasant environments.

⚡ How It Works

We've integrated real-time OpenStreetMap data with intelligent noise analysis to find the best route for you. The street network we download is automatically sized to your journey — so whether you're crossing a neighbourhood or a whole city, you always get complete coverage.

Our algorithm:

  • Analyzes every street in your area
  • Assigns noise costs based on road type
  • Finds the quietest possible path to your destination
  • Displays it beautifully on an interactive map

No ads. No tracking. Just pure, peaceful navigation.

🔧 Our Quiet Route Algorithm

1

Analyze Your Route

We calculate the real-world distance between your points and download a street network sized to fit — short trips get a tight graph, long routes get a wider one.

2

Assign Noise Costs

Each street gets a noise score from 1× (footpath) to 8× (motorway) based on its road type. Unknown roads default to 2.5× — never silently ignored.

3

Find Quiet Path

Our pathfinding algorithm calculates the route with the lowest total noise cost, not the shortest distance.

4

Navigate Peacefully

Follow the route on your map through the quietest streets available. If a location can't be found or no path exists, you'll get a clear, friendly message — not a crash.
